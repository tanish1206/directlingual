# Prohibited Items - FIFA World Cup 2026 Venue

The following items are strictly prohibited inside the stadium:

- **Weapons & Explosives**: Firearms, knives, scissors, fireworks, flares, pepper spray, or self-defense items.
- **Liquids & Containers**: Cans, glass bottles, thermos flasks, and open beverage containers. Empty clear plastic bottles up to 20 oz (approx 600ml) are permitted.
- **Banners & Signs**: Signs larger than 3' x 5' or containing offensive, political, or commercial content. Poles and sticks are prohibited.
- **Recording Devices**: Professional camera equipment (lenses longer than 6 inches), tripods, and drones. Action cameras (GoPros) and smartphones are allowed.
- **Noisemakers**: Air horns, whistles, vuvuzelas, megaphones, and sirens.
- **Strollers**: Large, non-collapsible strollers must be checked at the Stroller Check Stations near Gate A. Small collapsible strollers are allowed but must fit under your seat.
- **Animals**: Pets are prohibited. Qualified service animals trained to assist individuals with disabilities are welcome.
