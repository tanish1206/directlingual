# Medical & Security Emergencies

If you are experiencing or witnessing an active medical emergency or security threat:

1. **Immediate Assistance**:
   - Alert the nearest Stadium Steward, Host/Hostess, or Security Guard immediately. They are trained in emergency response and carry radios.
   - Text "HELP" to **69050** with your seat section, row, and problem for discrete security dispatch.

2. **First Aid Locations**:
   - **First Aid Station North**: Level 1 (Concourse 1) near Section 102.
   - **First Aid Station South**: Level 1 (Concourse 1) near Section 124.

3. **Emergency Evacuation**:
   - In the event of an evacuation, follow instructions over the public address system and directions from venue staff. Elevators will be reserved for guests with limited mobility.
