# Bag Policy - FIFA World Cup 2026 Venue

To ensure the safety and security of all fans, we enforce a strict bag policy:

1. **Approved Bags**:
   - Small clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags that do not exceed **12" x 6" x 12"** (30.5 x 15.2 x 30.5 cm).
   - One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags (Ziploc bag or similar).
   - Small clutch bags or purses no larger than **4.5" x 6.5"** (11.4 x 16.5 cm), with or without a handle or strap. These do not need to be clear.

2. **Prohibited Bags**:
   - Backpacks, large purses, coolers, briefcases, luggage, computer bags, fanny packs, and non-clear bags exceeding the clutch size limits are strictly prohibited.

3. **Exceptions**:
   - Medically necessary bags and diaper bags are permitted after inspection at designated lanes at Gate A and Gate C.
