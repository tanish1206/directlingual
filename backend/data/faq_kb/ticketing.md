# Ticketing Information - FIFA World Cup 2026 Venue

1. **Digital-Only Tickets**:
   - All tickets are mobile-only and must be displayed on a smartphone via the official FIFA World Cup App.
   - Screenshots and printed PDFs of tickets will not be accepted. We recommend downloading tickets to your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet before arriving to avoid issues with cellular reception.

2. **Ticket Resolution Desks**:
   - Located outside Gate A and Gate C. Open 4 hours prior to kickoff. Use these for login issues, lost devices, or transfer errors.

3. **Re-entry**:
   - Re-entry to the stadium is strictly prohibited. Once your mobile ticket is scanned and you exit, you cannot scan back in.
   
4. **Children's Tickets**:
   - All individuals, regardless of age (including infants in arms), must possess a valid ticket to enter the venue.
